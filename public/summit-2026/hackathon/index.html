The focus: preventing rheumatic heart disease

Acute rheumatic fever (ARF) can follow an untreated Group A Strep (Strep A) infection, and it can permanently damage the heart's valves. That lasting damage is rheumatic heart disease (RHD). In Australia, ARF and RHD persist almost entirely in Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities, because of continued high rates of Strep A exposure.

Stopping Strep A infections early is how you stop ARF and RHD. The SToPPING ARF trial is testing for Strep A at the point of care in remote communities, where sending samples to a lab is slow. Faster testing gives a clearer picture of where infections are happening, and that picture is what helps prevent them.

Read about the project at CSIRO.

The challenge

Teams spend the day using AI to build something that helps the people working on this problem. You'll work with a synthetic dataset made for the day, so no real patient data is used.

We'll share the full challenge brief on the day.
