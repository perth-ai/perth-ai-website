CP

Dr Camilo Pestana

Breaking AI: How to Hack Intelligent Systems
Abstract

What happens when someone deliberately tries to make an AI system behave in ways its developers never intended? AI applications introduce new attack surfaces that traditional software security practices don't fully address. From adversarial examples that fool computer vision models to prompt injection attacks that manipulate LLMs and autonomous agents, intelligent systems can fail in surprising ways. Drawing on his background in adversarial machine learning and AI security research, Camilo demonstrates how these attacks work, why they succeed, and what developers can do to defend against them — including why relying exclusively on model guardrails is insufficient. Expect technical demonstrations, real attack scenarios and practical lessons for building more secure AI systems. For AI engineers, security professionals, software developers and technical leaders deploying AI applications.

CM

Charlotte Mawle

AI Adoption & Change Management
Abstract

The AI you find exciting may be someone else's deepest fear. Organisations are investing heavily in AI tools, data and governance, yet most of the value is lost at the human layer: in how people experience AI as a threat to their identity, status and job security. Drawing on behavioural science and over 20 years leading change in mining, banking and government, Charlotte shows why smart people quietly resist good technology, why middle managers are where adoption is won or lost, and why poorly managed AI change is now a recognised psychosocial risk under WA's work health and safety framework. Tech leaders and implementers will leave with practical moves to design rollouts people lean into rather than brace against.

Bio

Charlotte Mawle helps organisations and the people in them navigate uncertainty, disruption and transformation, and today that work centres on AI adoption. She is the creator of the Conscious AI Adoption Blueprint™, a behavioural science grounded approach that addresses the human side of AI so investment turns into lasting value.

Over more than 20 years, Charlotte has led and supported complex transformation for organisations including BHP, Newmont Mining, Horizon Power, St John of God and Elsevier as well as coaching leaders in local and state government organisations. She introduced Lean Change Management to Australia and New Zealand in 2015 and is an executive coach and founder of Future Fit Females, a program helping women lead with confidence through midlife. Charlotte is based in Perth.
