⚡ Lightning Talks

Timing: TBC

Lightning talks run as two parallel tracks, in Tim Winton LT 213.101 and 213.104.

They follow the PechaKucha style: short, visual and kept moving by the slides, not the speaker.

The rules

  • 20 slides, 20 seconds each. That's 6 minutes 40 seconds in total.
  • Slides advance automatically. No clicker, no going back, no pausing. Talk to the slide that's on screen.
  • Pictures over words. Use images and keep text on slides to a minimum.
  • Rehearse with a timer. Twenty seconds goes quickly. It helps to know what you'll say on each slide.

Please send your slides to hello@perthai.org ahead of time so we can set them up to auto-advance.
