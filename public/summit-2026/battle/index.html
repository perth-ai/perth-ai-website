What it is

Early-stage start-ups, battling it out

The Start-up Battle is for the earliest start-ups in the room: no revenue yet, and some are still an idea. Up to 12 of them will demo at their tables through the day, and the top three get to pitch.

Come and see what they're building, ask hard questions and tell them what you think. At this stage, that's the most useful thing you can give them.
