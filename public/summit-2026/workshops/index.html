Two workshops, running side by side

A 2-Hour Speedrun of Yegge's 8 Levels of Agentic Coding

With Andy Spamer · 11:15 AM – 1:15 PM

Steve Yegge describes eight stages of agentic coding. They start at tab completion and stop at "building your own orchestrator." Andy speedruns all eight: optimising 1 agent, scaling to multiple agents, and finishing with your own custom harness.

Hands-on. Bring a laptop and an AI subscription.

Hackathon Prep Workshop

With Tim de Boer & Mash Prime · 11:15 AM – 1:15 PM

Mash & Tim bring this workshop targeted at helping you blitz the competition in our afternoon hackathon, and set you up for the life of an AI builder in the years beyond…! Learn from a selection of techniques customised to fit the group’s incoming skills. We’ll bring along an army of AI enabled assistants to help you at every stage.

The hackathon starts straight after, at 1:15 PM.
