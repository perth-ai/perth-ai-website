Exhibitors

Click a start-up to find out more about them and their giveaways.

  • madeAPT.
    MadeApt

    A Perth-founded fashion tech start-up where you design a garment and an independent tailor makes it to your measurements.

    They'll ask you one question: what's one piece of clothing you wish you could find? Then they'll hand you the keyboard. Describe your idea, watch it take shape in their AI Design Studio, which can turn garments into 3D, and try a change of your own. Start with wide-leg trousers, then see them with a higher waist or a different print.

    The AI part: generative AI turns a written description into a garment visual you can refine in your own words. If you can picture what you want but can't sketch it, it's a way to show a tailor what you mean. Customers have already taken designs from the studio through to paid orders.

    Prize: an A$250 MadeApt voucher for one attendee, to design their own garment and have it made to their measurements by one of MadeApt's independent tailors. You can start designing it at the booth on the day.

    Giveaway: tote bags made by local artists and tailors.

    Early stage · Founder Nina Karisik
